## ⚠️ Operational recommendations & deployment
- At edge (CDN/Gateway): Reject duplicate pagination parameters or canonicalize to a single `page` param (choose the first valid integer). Doing this at CDN prevents cache incoherency and repeated instrumentation across servers.
- For deep offsets: prefer keyset pagination (cursor-based) rather than OFFSET for pages > a safety threshold. Consider returning a "shallow empty" result when page > MAX_PAGE (for scrollable feed patterns).
- For user-specific feeds: mark `Cache-Control: private` or include identity in the CDN cache key (Vary or custom header) to avoid cross-user leakage.
- Always ensure `order_by()` includes a stable tiebreaker (id or created_at + id) when sorting on non-deterministic fields like `timestamp`.
- Run unit tests as a gate for new endpoint changes.
